## 🚀 What I Work On

### 📱 Mobile & Fintech (production)
Flutter apps running in the wild within the **Kkiapay / Opensi** ecosystem: payment terminals
(POS/TPE), merchant QR onboarding, NFC payments, and offline-first apps for field agents in
low-connectivity areas.

`MVVM (Stacked)` · `Repository pattern with sealed classes` · `Dio + Retrofit` · `Hive` ·
`Supabase / Firebase` · `Socket.IO` · `Shorebird OTA` · `Golden tests`

### 🛡️ Offensive Security
Full kill chain, always documented and reproducible: recon → enumeration → exploitation →
privilege escalation → write-up. Web exploitation, CVE research & PoC weaponization, Linux
privesc, applied crypto/JWT attacks, Wi-Fi (WPA2-Enterprise), red team (Sliver C2), and DFIR basics.

`nmap` · `ffuf` · `wpscan` · `Metasploit` · `linpeas` · `GTFOBins` · `jwt_tool` · `hashcat` · `Wireshark`
